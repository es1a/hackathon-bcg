Store weather and sensor readings separately, linked by location and time.

## 5. Most important feature: historical spatial intelligence

Your biggest differentiator is using previous farmer calls to identify emerging regional problems.

Example:

A farmer reports that crops are dying. The system finds that six other farmers within a 5 km radius reported similar symptoms during the last three days. Heavy rainfall was also recorded.

Instead of providing a generic response, the system could say:

> "Several nearby farmers have reported similar problems following heavy rainfall. There may be a regional water-quality issue. Avoid using the suspected source for irrigation until it has been assessed, and contact a local agricultural officer."

This should be presented as a possible issue, not a confirmed diagnosis.

Two critical implementation details: GPS coordinates cannot reliably be derived from the phone call itself, so ask for a village/location or use a registered farm location. Also, ensure the dashboard updates from the same SQL data used for recommendations, so the farmer response and officer view remain consistent.

For the hackathon: Prioritize one complete scenario: farmer speaks → problem gets stored → nearby reports are identified → weather and sensor data are considered → recommendation is spoken → the incident appears on the officer's map.
